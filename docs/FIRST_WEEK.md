# Your first week with FlipDesk

One small step a day. Each one takes 10-30 minutes, and FlipDesk ticks it off on its own.

## Day 1: Add 5 items
Open **Shoot** on your phone (or **New listing** on a computer). Take 3-6 photos per item: front, back, tag, any flaws. The AI writes the title, description, details and a price. Check the "double-check" notes, then save. Coming from another tool? Use **Switch to FlipDesk** to import instead.

## Day 2: Crosslist them to 3 or more sites
Open an item and tick the sites (your favorites are already ticked), then **Crosslist**. eBay, Etsy and Shopify list right away; Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Grailed, Facebook and Whatnot go through the Chrome extension. More sites = more buyers seeing it. When it sells anywhere, FlipDesk delists it everywhere else.

## Day 3: Turn on phone alerts
Install the free **ntfy** app, subscribe to a long private topic name, and enter the same name in **Settings → Phone notifications**. You'll hear about every sale and any oversell right away.

## Day 4: Set a weekly goal
On the **Dashboard**, set goals like "list 20 items, $300 profit". FlipDesk shows if you're on pace and tells you each day what keeps you there.

## Day 5: Make a bundle
Open **Bundles**. FlipDesk suggests natural lots (same brand or size, slow items first). A bundle moves stale stock and raises your average sale.

## Day 6: Move a slow item
Open **Power tools**: send an offer to watchers, or take the suggested price drop on something that's been sitting. The **Today** card on the dashboard picks the best one for you.

## Day 7: Look at your numbers
Open **Reports**: profit and return by source, brand and category, and which sites make you the most. Buy more of what works; skip what doesn't.

## After week one
Check the dashboard's **Today: do these 3** each day, keep shooting new items, and read your Monday summary. Stuck on anything? Press **❓ Help**.
